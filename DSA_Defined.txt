###
Data Structures

A data structure is a way of organizing and storing data to enable efficient access and modification.
The choice of a data structure depends on the type of operations required, such as searching, sorting, insertion, or deletion. Examples include arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, and graphs. 
Efficient data structures help minimize time and space complexities, making programs faster and more memory-efficient.

###

Algorithms

An algorithm is a step-by-step procedure or set of instructions designed to solve a specific problem or perform a task.
Algorithms often operate on data structures to achieve their goals.
Examples include sorting algorithms (e.g., Quick Sort, Merge Sort) and searching algorithms (e.g., Binary Search). 
The efficiency of an algorithm is measured in terms of time complexity and space complexity, often expressed using Big O Notation.
